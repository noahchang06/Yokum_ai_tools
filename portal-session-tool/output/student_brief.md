# UNC/SDSS Student Action Brief

## What This Project Is About
DNCR wants to estimate the economic impact of its natural and cultural sites across North Carolina. The project would look at statewide impact and local impact for individual sites such as state parks, historic sites, aquariums, museums, and the zoo.

## Why It Matters for North Carolina
DNCR says its sites are important economic anchors, especially in rural communities. Better analysis could help the state understand how public cultural and natural resources contribute to local economies, jobs, visitor spending, and tax revenue.

## Useful Skills
- Data cleaning
- Spreadsheet work
- Python or R
- GIS or mapping
- Survey design
- Dashboard or chart design
- Policy writing
- Economic impact analysis
- Careful documentation of uncertainty and missing data

## Beginner Entry Points
- Clean monthly visitation data
- Create a data dictionary for site-level fields
- Summarize existing reports from aquariums, state parks, the zoo, or other examples
- Build simple charts of visitation by month or site type
- Help review survey questions for clarity
- Track what information is missing or inconsistent

## Advanced Entry Points
- Link site coordinates to county tourism or demographic data
- Analyze seasonal visitation patterns across site types
- Compare economic impact methods used by other states or cultural institutions
- Build a prototype dashboard or site-level fact sheet
- Help design a visitor survey sampling plan
- Explore procurement data by vendor location if those fields are available

## Possible Student Contributions
Students could help prepare data, create exploratory visuals, review economic impact examples, support survey design, document limitations, and produce draft materials that help researchers and DNCR staff decide on a feasible project scope.
