# Researcher Brief

## Research Opportunity
DNCR is looking for a comprehensive economic impact assessment of its sites and possibly its programs. The main opportunity is to design a credible method that can handle many different types of public assets, including parks, museums, aquariums, historic sites, the zoo, and grant programs.

The transcript suggests a practical research need rather than a purely academic one: DNCR wants evidence that can inform budget, fundraising, marketing, and strategic investment decisions.

## Possible Research Questions
- What is the statewide economic impact of DNCR sites?
- What is the local economic impact of individual DNCR sites?
- How much visitor spending, job support, economic output, and tax revenue can reasonably be attributed to DNCR sites?
- How should economic impact be estimated when visitation methods vary across divisions?
- Can program and grant impacts be estimated in addition to physical site impacts?
- How much procurement activity stays in North Carolina or local communities?
- What survey design would produce useful visitor data during the Memorial Day to Labor Day window?

## Data Needs
Known data sources include monthly visitation data, operational budgets, site coordinates, grant awards by program and county, VisitNC county tourism data, Hub spend data, and existing internal or external economic impact reports.

Additional data needs include clearer procurement data, supplier zip codes, visitor origin or zip code, party size, how visitors heard about the site, spending behavior, survey methodology, and consistent definitions across site types.

## Possible Methods
- Economic impact modeling using visitor counts, spending assumptions, and local geography
- Site-level fact sheets modeled after examples such as Florida state park impact summaries
- Survey design and sampling guidance for peak-season visitors
- Geospatial matching of DNCR sites to county tourism, demographic, or economic data
- Procurement leakage analysis using vendor location and purchase amount
- Sensitivity analysis to show how results change under different visitor spending assumptions

These methods are conditional on data access and quality. The transcript does not specify a required model or software.

## Feasibility Risks
Visitation data is collected differently across divisions, which may make comparisons difficult. DNCR does not currently collect demographic information uniformly. Procurement data with useful vendor geography may or may not be available. Program-level impact is likely more complex than site-level impact and is described as secondary.

The summer survey timing creates urgency: DNCR wants feedback quickly enough to deploy from Memorial Day to Labor Day.

## Questions Before Committing
- What is the required final deadline?
- Which sites are highest priority if all 100-plus sites cannot be analyzed deeply?
- What exact data files can DNCR provide, and in what format?
- Are individual site reports required for every site or only a subset?
- What decisions must the results support?
- Does DNCR need a defensible economic impact estimate, a planning tool, or both?
- How should the project handle programs and grants if site analysis is the must-have?
