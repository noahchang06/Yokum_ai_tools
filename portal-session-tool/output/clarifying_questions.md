# Clarifying Questions

## For the Agency
- What final decisions should this analysis support: budget requests, fundraising, marketing, operations, or all of these?
- What is the deadline for a first useful product?
- Which sites are highest priority if the team cannot produce detailed reports for every site?
- Should the first phase focus only on physical sites, or should grant programs be included?
- What level of precision is needed for the economic impact estimates?
- Who inside DNCR will review and use the final outputs?

## For Researchers
- What economic impact method is most appropriate for DNCR's mix of parks, museums, aquariums, historic sites, and the zoo?
- How should inconsistent visitation-count methods be handled?
- What assumptions about visitor spending are acceptable?
- Can existing reports from aquariums, the zoo, state parks, Florida State Parks, or Americans for the Arts be reused as models?
- What data would be required to estimate local tax revenue and jobs supported?
- How should uncertainty be communicated in public-facing outputs?

## For Students
- What data files can students access safely?
- Are there entry-level tasks such as data cleaning, charting, or literature review?
- What skills are most needed first: GIS, survey design, data cleaning, economic modeling, or writing?
- Can students help test the summer survey before deployment?
- What products would be useful for students to produce in a short course or project timeline?

## For Implementation
- What format will DNCR provide the visitation, budget, grant, location, and procurement data in?
- Can procurement data include purchase amount, vendor name, and vendor zip code?
- What survey platform will be used: Microsoft Forms, Jotform, Smartsheet, or something else?
- How will survey collection be randomized across sites and days?
- What privacy or data-sharing constraints apply?
- What should count as a minimum viable product?
