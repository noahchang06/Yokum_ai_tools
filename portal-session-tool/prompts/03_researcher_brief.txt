Create a researcher-facing brief from the transcript and extraction.

Use exactly these sections:

# Researcher Brief
## Research Opportunity
## Possible Research Questions
## Data Needs
## Possible Methods
## Feasibility Risks
## Questions Before Committing

Rules:
- Write for university researchers deciding whether the project is feasible and interesting.
- Identify research opportunities without overstating what the transcript supports.
- Separate available data from data that still needs to be requested.
- Do not invent methods that require unavailable data unless clearly labeled as conditional.
- If something is missing, write "not specified" or "unclear from transcript."
